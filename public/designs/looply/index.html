Classic cold email tools focus on sending more. Looply connects prospect discovery, ICP scoring, enrichment, personalized sequences, sender routing and replies in one workspace.

That means you can build smaller, better-qualified audiences and give every email the context it needs before a campaign starts.

  • Founders who want pipeline without a large SDR team
  • Sales teams running personalized cold email
  • Agencies managing outreach for multiple clients

If cold email is part of your acquisition strategy and control matters as much as scale, Looply is a fit.

Looply continuously monitors buying signals like keyword posts, engagement with your page, a competitor's page or an influencer you pick, recent role changes and new funding rounds. Each lead is enriched, matched against your ICP and scored, and only high-intent profiles are engaged.

Looply gives teams control over connected senders, campaign pacing, follow-up timing, routing and approvals. Deliverability still depends on your domains, authentication, audience, content and sending practices, so Looply keeps those decisions visible instead of hiding them behind a volume-only workflow.

Cold email performance depends on your ICP, offer, list quality, deliverability and messaging. Looply helps you measure campaign progress, replies, sender health and contact quality so you can improve the inputs that drive meetings. It does not promise a fixed reply or booking rate.
