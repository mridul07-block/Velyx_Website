(07) FAQ

Before you book.

How can you ship in 10 days?+

Tight scope, one decision-maker, and daily async updates. We cut the meetings, not the craft.

Do you also build, or only design?+

Both. We design in Figma and build in Framer or Next.js, depending on what your team needs.

What if I don't like the direction?+

You review at every stage. If day 3 isn't right, we change course before anything gets built.

Can I edit the site myself later?+

Yes. You get full ownership and a short video walkthrough of how to update everything.
